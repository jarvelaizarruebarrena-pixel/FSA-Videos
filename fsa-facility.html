FSA Outdoor Facility

A 1.5 acre lot with a natural grass field (goals facing each other end to end), painted sidelines, team benches and bleachers. Shipping container FSA office, coffee shop and storage room, a covered reception, a sand court reached from the walkway, a small park and 30 parking spaces. Every room can be entered.
